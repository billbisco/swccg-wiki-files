Besieged
review by Jeff Hansen
[click here to view card]

This Effect deploys on a captured starship to allow your characters that are present with it to battle the Light Side characters that are trapped inside as if they were present together at a site. Besieged is then canceled when the captured starship either escapes or is stolen. This allows you to steal starships that have characters aboard them, and is extremely powerful when you can mercilessly spank a lone pilot.
