Undercover

"Good Evening" written vertically. These words, along with the profile shadow on the wall, are a reference to the classic TV series "Alfred Hitchcock Presents." At the beginning of the show, Hitchcock's shadow, in profile, would appear on a wall. When introducing the show, he always began with the words "Good evening."
