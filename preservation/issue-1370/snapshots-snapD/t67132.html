    Post by DDM » September 14th, 2017, 5:58 am

    EC 2017 was a blast.
    I had no real hope of going to top 8 (it’s been nearly 2-3 years I have very little time for SWCCG, and I dust off my cards just to participate in the EC), but that was the same last year and still I made it.
    The 2 weeks of preparation I had this year were busy building decks for Max, who is even more out of the game than me and make him play them.

    For decks I was still quite confident about Hyperdeal (which no one seems to play, but is pretty good), as for LS, I was a bit at a loss.
    EBO Ketwol wasn’t effective as before, since the new rulings about shields allowed to pack all shields, so I couldn’t count much on players not packing the shield anymore.
    Also, EOPs seemed a pretty tough match-up in this regard. It was a near autowin last year, but Slave I and the flipped version made everything different.

    In the end, I ended up with a Ketwol/EBO Speeders/Capitals, a bit more standard than you could expect, if it weren’t for Revolution (you can’t play the Ketwol shields AND the Revolution shield in the same game) and Redemption non V.

    The main issue was work (real life work) which prevented me to take days free, so I drove 11 hours from Milano on Friday, and had planned to leave Sunday afternoon unless I managed to get into the semi finals / finals.
    I arrive at the venue late (11pm) and we have fun, drink and talk, and people say they don’t want to play Emil Game 1 while actually I’m pretty much ok with it (because if I lose, it should be still good for SoS, and if I win, what the heck, it’s even better).

    Before going to sleep I realize that I can throw out the Blockade Bridge off my DS deck, the 2 WMAOP, and just add a Cor: BD (more reliable activation, since I have already the Exe:DB and I start Mob Points in the deck), Crash Landing and EPP 4-LOM V.
    Thankfully Patrick (Hungary) has a DB to spare.

    The next morning, pairings are announced…

    Game 1: my Hyperdeal vs Emil’s Old Allies

    :D
    I can’t help but laugh. Speak about a coincidence. At least I’m playing DS, but I have very few experience vs Old Allies (the last games online went pretty badly) and I know it can be quite tough. Add the fact that it’s Emil and the result is bad since he’s not only good but also thinks out of the box for deckbuilds (like me).
    I start the usual, that is Drop V, Endor Shield V, Mob Points.
    The game starts very fast.
    I don’t remember what we had on table (I knew I had the Exe), but he sets up with a loaded Falcon in space (taking off) and has Harc Self V + Rey on a BG site at Jakku.
    I have 4 cards in hand, and one of those is EPP Mara.
    I think a while, and guess correctly his play: he knows I have only 4 cards in hand and probably play Walkers/Veers, so the odds of me having an EPP are small, and this would explain his play.
    Also, I think that I can’t just play “safe” with Emil, that is, without taking risks or gambles. I usually play that way, but I know he’s an excellent deckbuilder, so if I leave him time to set up, even if I think I have a Control deck, he might end up being the one controlling my deck instead. I think it through and know my deck has a good quick set up too and good destinies, so I should bank on it and put back some pressure on him.
    So I do not pay to drain (it would have been lowered to 1) and drop EPP Mara, he plays Antilles Maneuver V (thank god I didn’t drain), we clear the site (his flipped side didn’t matter), making him flip back and forces him to draw the next turn to find back some cards to flip again.
    Due to him starting the Profundity effect, I can’t really move to Jakku to contest his drain 2 yet, with a loaded Falcon already there, not when I’m draining 2 thanks to Chiraneau too at Bespin.
    We exchange drains, he flips again on Jakku… details are blurried in my mind, but I’m draining for 4 (B2+Veers on his 2/2 site, which is a bad choice, since it is on the far side of the table, it would have been better to deploy it in the middle), but of course he’s killing a drain with his flipped side to 1 so we’re basically trading 3 damage a turn.
    Then since he has General Leia on table, which kills my AO, I know it’s a question of time before Veers dies too (even if I have a Command in hand) so I decide to reinforce him. I have B4 in hand but nothing worwhile to pull from the deck, and a Trample in hand.
    He has General Leia and Rey on the 2/1. I drop B4 in front of them. My play is to trample Leia and kill Rey with minimal losses (B4), and then recur Trample with B2 (V) the next turns, or force Emil to grab, which would allow me to move the Executor in front of Falcon and resist with recurring Imperial Commands. If I miss the trample, no biggie, I can just move away or reinforce with Veers/B2, and in case move Exe too just in case for shuttling some back up. What I was missing is that I forgot that Leia is ability 5 (I’m so used to Leia being abilty 4, and her card is hidden behind Rey).
    So I drop B4 and I play Trample. Emil asks “on who ?” and I’m like “does it really matter, I still need a 5”. Not really. Heh. So I’m thinking a bit about what to do. Trampling Rey would be easier but would leave me open to Rebel Leadership (V) from Emil, and I don’t like it at all (which is a bit stupid, because even with Leadership V, I shouldn’t risk overflow that much).
    Since he doesn’t grab it, I decide to aim for Leia anyway. Odds are way lower (I have a lot of 5s, but only 2 7s and 1 6s), but I don’t really care, since I can pull Trample back, but if I manage, it’s really great. I have 2 “7s” in the deck, and I draw the destiny and … it’s a 7. There was no tracking involved, just blind luck.
    Emil is like wtf, and says this changes completely the game.
    And it does. Since Leia dies, I decide to battle Rey, and we clear each other, which means he flips back, again.
    We continue moving our pieces around but time is going to get called, and I have a lead of 2 cards, so I just move everything in front of his people / space, and I keep recycling my last cards with Drop V, at more or less the same time where I had the answers to deal with his space (gun, tracked 7, They’ve Shut Down the Main Reactor).
    The game ends with me winning on time.
    This game wasn’t “won” on time though, but on that blind destiny 7.
    The reality was even more grim: he had They’re Tracking Us V in hand, so only a 7 would have made it. Also, if I kill Rey instead and battle Leia, he had Protector in hand, which meant react with EPP Chewie, potential Leadership (V) in hand, shooting, so a total of 12+2BD for him against my 8+1 BD and 0 forfeit.
    Not that I thought about it, at that time, but this shows you actually how this was the turning point of the game.

    My thoughts:
    I played pretty well that game, but it was not a game that’s going to repeat itself (even me playing aggressively).
    I was pretty much impressed with his OA deck. It’s a beast, and very well thought of.
    I don’t know much OA a lot, but indeed it’s pretty strong. In the hands of Emil, it’s even worse.


    Game 2: my EBO Bat-Ket (Wokling V, ANSB, Strike Planning) vs Koen’s Court

    So Koen is the ONLY person at the EC who knows my decklist. What are the odds, again ?
    Court could be winnable, but then, maybe not. It all depends on the cards in hand I get.
    Basically, we all play safe. I Wokling Projection his AC, and keep losing 1 to his objective, because I don’t want to risk dropping my speeders at the Pit (this is due to lack of testing … I suppose I can do that in a way, as long as I have Wedge/General on a speeder with Leadership V) or anywhere for that matter. My goal is in the beginning to set up Haven + fleet on Hoth and drain 3. I’ll get ping by 1 but I’m winning.
    Koen needs indeed to do stuff so he deploys in space at Nal Hutta with zuckuss + epp 4-lom, and another ship. I feel a bit better, so I drop wedge+general in speeder at 4th marker.
    Now I’m draining 5, he is damaging 2. He immediately drop someone else at another tat BG site, and pulls resistance. So now I’m damaging 4 to his 3, it’s way more even.
    I make a blunder at a certain time, playing On The Edge because there is no Secret Plans out or Ketwol shield, thinking I can net a free retrieval that way (and then play it again with Ketwol), but he grabs simply On The Edge with his grabber. And all this for a tracked 4, so no real gain there.
    The issue mainly is that I can’t find other ships. I have some at Haven, but no big ones. I could move in front of him but that’s asking for Boba to be added up and him drawing 4 BDs, and I can’t find any copy of leadership. When I eventually find it, we’re in the last turns before time is called (I couldn’t draw too much either before that), and Koen in the meantime has been playing very well, bringing lone guys under the shield have with his 3 barriers, blocking any attempt to kill them (I had grabbed something else).
    He ends up with some last turn battles, making him win the game on time, but it wasn’t by just 1-2 cards, due to the retrieval.

    My thoughts:
    Well played by Koen, he knew the deck and played it safe, and knowing when to push too.
    I start to feel a bit unconvinced about my LS deck, but hey, it was vs Koen.


    Game 3: my EBO Bat-Ket vs Kevin Jaap EOPs with Combat Response / Est Control V

    EOPs should be “winnable”.
    In theory.
    Kevin starts with Emperor, and then he ends up at the DB. A move that I like because I have the possibility of beating down the Emperor with Luke + speeder. Something he can’t see coming because I don’t start Man. Flaps V. I have however a Rogue 1 in hand, so I get out Echo Base Garrison, Cmdr Luke V, and vlam, here goes Luke in speeder. I don’t draw very high (double 3s) but he doesn’t either (a 3 too) so he has to lose Emperor or 5. He peels 5, pulls the 1/1 site where no vehicles can go (damned, this changes everything!), deploy a ship (slave I I think) to Endor and moves Emperor away. Of course, he also pulled before Force Lightning.
    So the good start is now blocked, because he will drain 2+2 and I have to pay to drain 1.
    I get up my fleet, Wedge in rogue in 4th marker, and start running around him in space. We are trading drains, but when he pulls a new ship, he just spreads them out, and again I don’t have very big ships that can stand on their own if divided and I’m not drawing any. This means I rapidly lose Haven, which is ok.
    He tries sometimes to block the Wedge drain with a big main, and it doesn’t go so badly, but not very well either with him, thanks to Insertion Planning.
    In the end, I lose the game again on time, but not before I had to make a risky move to try to get back into it (spreading my fleet and drawing into cards to find Order To Engage, him being shield busted, but it was of course one of the few cards in the USED pile).
    I end up losing by a few cards, and of course On The Edge was eluding me all game. :D

    My thoughts:
    So I win against Emil and then I lose 2 games in a row.
    Funny. :D
    I am now utterly convinced my LS deck sucks, but I suppose Kevin played very very well, and kept a high pressure also thanks to Ominours Rumors all game (which I forgot was still working even after I flipped him back by invading the bunker).


    Game 4: Hyperdeal vs Nelson’s TRM (with Sai’Tor V)

    Technically a bad match-up for me. It was a very good match-up years ago, when I had 2 Tie Defender (Mareek could pull one, and B4 could pull Mareek) to deal vs DDTA shield, but here, I have only Sith Infiltrator as a second ship (which was added due to Tatooine Ep1 AND for TRM).
    It’s not unwinnable though.
    My deck starts a 2/1 with -1 drain for LS, so it’s not TOO bad.
    Nothing happens turn 1 but him starting to outactivate me. I just activate, pulls my location, and keep an eye to see if a ship ends up in my Force pile.
    Turn 2 he drops Jedi Luke on my site + saber. I end up thankfully activating enough and seeing that the Executor is in my force pile, so I draw him, getting too Tarkin’s Orders.
    He drains and adds another Jedi + LS.
    I drop Exe, Chiraneau, and since I have Ghhhk I just leave Adm Piett alone at a DB for activation.
    I cancel his drain (he grabs). Next turn I drop B2 + Veers at the DB and I move Piett over. He adds Lightsaber Prof with Wokling V. Now he threatens a drain 4. I manage to verify his deck and I see no space, and I curse myself for throwing away at a drain Maul ship, which could have satisfied Resistance.
    I just eat the drain, and manages to find Sense and U-3PO. The Droid goes down, I’m not sure if he tries a Sorry About the Mess (which gets Sensed) or not, but basically this means game over, because he can’t spread (I did fortify a bit) and if he moves everything I just follow with U-3PO and then move away.
    I also manage to start abusing a recurring Trample, with a 7 tracked, killing Jedi Luke then Anakin.
    In all this I also retrieved Maul’s Ship, but no need to deploy him there (can’t find Maul anyway), which is good, because he has to make a move and drops 2 ships (wedge in ship + GliG1 V) there, which would have been horrible for me, to drain there more, and to goad me into battling him there so that he could have R3 Falcon for more beatdown.
    Seeing he can’t do much, he concedes a few turns later, which his ground risking also to get crushed after too many tramples.

    My thoughts:
    Vs TRM it’s a question of luck and card drawing. If my ships keep staying in Reserve, I lose, simple as that. If I find them quickly, it’s harsh but usually quite winnable. Here Nelson was also playing a lot of sabers, so he was putting more pressure (which most TRM don’t, on ground).


    Game 5: Hyperdeal vs Coruscant Jedi Chamber Mains

    I have not much recollection of that game.
    I remember my opponent pulling Yoda and getting also C&TV with it. So I was afraid of a beatdown and I played quite safe, not leaving a lone guy at the 1/0 DB unless I drew the Ghhhk.
    He was playing also Frozen Assets IIRC.
    This game turned out in the end a bit like the TRM match-up, just with the fact that I had no issue setting up Executor, but he was draining for A LOT at my sites with sabers, so I had to move B4+fodder, EPP Mara, B2+Veers in front and I nearly lost the game there.
    He played an Under Attack (I think I grabbed) and I sensed it, saving B4+fodder (which prevented him from clearing the site). So in the end he lost some guys, I lost EPP Mara, B4 and fodder, leaving me with a lone B2+Veers, but at least I killed two of his guys with sabers.
    The scary thing is that I saw another Under Attack in his reserve, so he was playing 2…
    Here again, recurring Trample and/or U-3PO saved the day.


    Game 6: EBO Bat-Ket vs King Jon IG-88 with soooOOOO much force LOSS

    This game is crazy.
    When Jon asks me for a Rebel, I know what’s going to happen, I just don’t remember if it actually matters what rebel I give him.
    And indeed it doesn’t matter.
    Jon is playing Aiiigg Aaaaaghhh!! x5. Basically, the concept is simple: you want to beat him ? You need to play good characters. Good characters have ability 3+. Well, for every one of those on table, you’re going to lose 1 Force.
    Be it Rebel, Resistance, whatever. And then, why not, he can battle you with Scum and retrieve the interrupt back.
    Thankfully he tries to pull the AC turn 1 and … misses … so I see EXACTLY what he’s up to.
    I ponder a plan in my head: Grab this interrupt, for sure, but also find my Sense.
    I know that to win, I need to find my big ships and not use characters … so every turn, I’m attentive to what is left in my reserve.
    Turn 2 all is perfect, Sense and some big ships are in my force pile so I just draw all.
    In the end I manage to set up Home 1, Falcon + Han, and a third ship at Haven.
    He plays the interrupt, I grab and Sense, which goes through. I don’t remember if the other character was Gen Leia (near auto Sense) or Madine, but I remember having a majority of 1-2 in reserve. Anyway, the Sense goes through.
    For all the game, I keep these 2 only characters of ability 3+ on table, so after a while he has no choice but to hit me for 2 instead of not doing anything with his interrupt.
    We’re trading drains, of course, similar to the game with Koen, and near the end of the game he manages to pull Carbon Freezing Testing, threathening another 8 Force Loss. We make some calculus and I see that while I’m winning, with the 8 Force loss he might be the one getting the upper hand.
    Time is getting close, I am not going to stall, so I end my next-to-last turn with 30 seconds left while he’s being very nervous about me not letting him having another turn.
    He hits me with Carbon Freezing, adding 2, I don’t have They’re Tracking Us V in hand.
    That puts me down 2 under him and I lose On The Edge and Collision from Reserve. Collision is the last card lost.
    He deploys and battles, and retrieves at the North Ridge, but Insertion Planning it ends up a no-win situation for him ; he moves then his fleet in front of me.
    I know there is a 6 in bottom of my reserve (Insertion Planning). I have Redemption in hand and Rey on the Tank.
    I take a desperate gamble. I Ketwol Collision, leaving 2 in reserve, and try a Collision … I draw a 3 (Ellorrs) …
    Damned, here goes my first attempt at a beatdown. The Collision move was actually worthless since I should have know it was a 3 (since I suppose I drew that card for BD last turn).
    But not all is lost. I drop Redemption at the system where he has 2 ships, drop Rey aboard for free, get back Ellorss, and fight. He draws high (5) too.
    In the end, when the smoke clears, he loses all +3 cards, so that makes us even (since I had one less Life Force due to Ellorrs).
    We count lost piles, I have less in Lost Pile and I win that way.
    Pheww.

    My thoughts:
    I made some mistakes especially in the end … If I had Madine on table (not sure) I could have played Solo’s ability for On The Edge from lost pile, to retrieve 5 and win the game that way without risking a battle.
    I loved Jon’s deck. Very very clever.


    Game 7: EBO Bat-Ket vs Emil ISB

    This is tough. We are both 4-2, so the one who loses is probably out.
    The bad news is that I’m playing my LS, which won only one game as of now, and on Lost Pile count :D.
    The good news is that I kept Blount in the deck, also to deal vs ISB, but it doesn’t work THAT much if he plays Coruscant system (which could be done, depending on the builds).
    But he plays the 2/0 Coruscant site.
    The game is very technical and attentive the first turns. ISB is slow, for sure, and I’m positive he doesn’t play Decree non V, so he can’t ignore me too much. EBO can be slow too to set up (or quick, if you wish it so).
    What happens is that turn 2 he keeps quite some force active. I could technically drop Wedge in speeder + General + Leadership V in hand, or do the same with Haven + EBO on table with one ship, but that means a possible beatdown on my forces. So I just set up EBO and Bacta Tank, and draws some more, leaving some active.
    If he keeps piling up force, I can set up pretty well in space (especially because now I was attentive and drew all my big ships) with Haven (which I pulled) and be in a good spot.
    He drops 2 AT-Ats and some 4-5 ISB, and flips. He has also out Vader + shuttle + Black 2 + pilot all at the other DB, and he takes off at Fondor. So now he will be draining 2+1.
    With most of his forces somewhere else, I drop Wedge combo at the north ridge, my fleet at Hoth/Haven, and pass, drawing some more.
    We trade drains the next turn (him 3, me 5), he adds up a 3rd AT-AT at another DB, increasing his drain potential to 5.
    I then just play Blount to his 2/0, flip him back, add Cmdr Luke and Rogue 1, and Blount hops aboard there. Now he’s draining only 3 (but I do projection one of the DBs IIRC so that’s 2), while I drain for 7 (capped at 6 with Resistance).
    To make things worse, I think we exchange a battle with Profundity / Ackbar V from hand + GliG1 + Redemption vs his Vader/Black 2 combo (with no force active), but he still draws with Black 2 pilot, and subtracts quite a bit. I lose a guy, while he loses Black 2 + pilot to battle damage/attrition.
    In the meantime Emil is preparing his play. He has no much left (characters) in hand, most are on table, so Tarkin V alone goes vs Rogue 1, and he plays Nevar Yalnal and kills Blount. He flips ISB. He doesn’t have another ISB to clear me though.
    I try to save Blount with the Tank, but he plays Cold Feet (V). Applause.
    I then have no choice but to Wokling V Blount but I don’t have the force IIRC (last turn battle was all I had to use in space). He moves all his fleet in front of Profundity.
    So next LS turn I retrieve him with Wokling V which slows me down a turn, but whatever.
    I don’t remember what I did then, but I remember having used quite some force for some stuff (probably a battle on ground to clear one of his DBs because I don’t want to take another drain 2, maybe with Leia, he grabs Leadership V), and I start moving my 3 ships away from his fleet. When I start moving the last one (GliG1) I realize my critical mistake (I should have moved the small ships first), and indeed he gravity shadows it. Thankfully, I kept some force active and so I move Home One + another ship in front, and some forfeit fodder.
    I forgot however to battle the lone Tarkin that turn (I knew he had a Ghhhk, I just forgot to do it) so actually that force that I didn’t use saved me, and allowed me one more movement for shuttling up some more fodder.
    Next turn he adds Flagship Exe (oh god), battles, and when the smoke clears I lost all but no battle damage (or maybe 1, not sure).
    I get back Blount with Madine, kill Tarkin (Ghhhk), flip him back for good. I am continuously draining 2 at the North ridge all game, and I move again my fleet away to Endor.
    Without ISB retrieval, he is pinging for 1 and 1, while I’m doing way more damage.
    Time is near the end, we have both a couple of turns more, but there’s not much he can do to prevent me from winning this game (this time, something around 5-7 force difference).

    My thoughts:
    I made again some mistakes here, but not too many, and I played imho very well, to deal vs his deck. It was quite chess-like, and I knew I could damage him quite a while and he couldn’t really do much once he was unflipped (but I was wrong, with this Nevar Yalnal … oh boy…).
    We talk a bit after the game about how it could have done differently, but there wasn't much, but I understand the power of his ISB (in battles) that didn't really showed up vs me.
    Krypto also looks at me like it was the first time he sees me when he gets to know I just beat Emil, AGAIN. :D thanks Krypto, really.


    Game 8: Hyperdeal vs Koen’s RST

    Again Koen. At least, he doesn’t know my DS deck.
    I had built Joe Olson’s RST for Max, so I knew what RST could do. It is not an easy match-up, but I start IAO&SP so that helps.
    My strategy vs this is start Imp. Decree V instead of Drop! V.
    Drop V is important for general battling, but here, dropping down the damage received is way more important.
    In test games, I even managed to drop the damage to 4 by holding all Bespin locations.

    Koen, seeing IAO&SP, is actually keeping all his options open. He doesn’t drop Harc Self V immediately, but just sets up some guys at the Landing site. He also plays quite quickly Perimeter Scan V to play another shield, which imho is a mistake when you still have nearly all your shield pulls.
    I manage eventually to set up Exe and I have a Walker on the CC: DB. Not sure what that walker was, but maybe just B4 with Vader/Mara.
    He drops Harc Self V (I make him pay +1) and start paying more but I stop him immediately and play Trample. He ponders if to grab or not but decides not to.
    I draw U-3PO for destiny and he’s dead. We “argue” a bit about if Trample has been errataed to target Defense value instead of Ability, and ask for a rule verification on that. It still targets Ability so Harc Self V’s armor of 3 doesn’t matter and he dies.
    Beatdown #1 avverted. :D
    Koen has no choice but to then try to blow up the bunker, and he prepares himself that way, with one charge.
    My memory is a bit fuzzy here. I don’t remember much, because I played vs RST Day 3 too (vs Jimmy).
    I think he had quite low destinies in his deck, so I felt fine. Then he drew the only 5s in his deck and it blew up. Meh.
    He tries another time to make a beatdown but thanks to B2 and pulling Trample, Harc Self V dies again before calling back up.
    Beatdown #2 also avverted. :D
    I’m not sure if I eventually manage to block his drain with u-3PO or if I just shoot and kill his Home One. Koen will probably remember. Point is, I am outdamaging him, and he has less life force than me, so he eventually concedes.

    My thoughts:
    I’m probably in, and I can’t believe it. Nothing went wrong in this game, and I have the confirmation that my Hyperdeal can work quite well vs. RST.




    The evening:
    Tamas is being annoying but he eventually has to give the top 8, and yes, I’m in, but barely, as the last 6-2. I have to play Jimmy tomorrow, and I like this, because I just like him and he’s a great deckbuilder too.
    I know my EBO autoloses to his Invasion Blasters, though.
    Not sure about Hyperdeal vs Hyperdrive … I suppose I can win that.
    Anyway, I ponder if to play RST instead, but meh.
    I manage to fall asleep at 2 am with no LS deck, just an idea that I can gamble he doesn’t play Invasion, and swapping some cards for Monnok / Grimtaash, due to Chris's advice.

    In the morning, I can’t sleep and I end up waking up at 6.
    I have a revelation however. I could play my last year EBO Squassin, with Tydirium and Blount. This will basically guarantee me a quick/surprise attack on Naboo.
    Also, I still add a Grimtaash in the deck.

    Day 3: my Hyperdeal vs Jimmy’s RST

    I feel ok in this match-up, IF it is the same deck as Joe. Point is: it’s not, and I don’t get to know until it’s too late.
    I do not have a Free exe effect either, but I have a Force Push V. Turn 1 nothing worthwhile (like a free effect) is in my Force Pile so I can’t do much, so I just drop Piett at the Exe DB, trying to force him to play DDTA or to burn cards to attack him.
    He ponders a bit, drop Madine, and says he has Blount. Point is, if he drops Blount and something else, I still can “burn” Decree V to draw a BD, and leave him with a lone guy at the Exe:DB and Madine all alone at the Landing site.
    Indeed he must think about it, and just sets up at the landing site. I think he also plays grabs Ackbar V with Leadership (I grab).
    I activate, and this time 4 Free effects are in my force pile. I Force Push for it, but this means I have to put down Sense because all other cards are/seem way important.
    This will bite me in the ass later on.
    I set up with Executor, which costs me 2 due to the free effect, and makes my first mistake by not looking closely enough at my Force Pile before, meaning B4 goes down at the CC:DB but pulls only EPP Mara, and not EPP Vader.
    He just drops down Home One with Ackbar at Endor. I’m draining for 3, him for 2, but he generates more force and still I didn’t see Harc Self V. So I solidify at the CC:DB because I can’t find my Trample.
    Jimmy just drops a ton of guys to the Home One and move over to Bespin.
    This is where it gets ugly.
    He has more power than me, Ackbar, potential leadership V, AND also Sabine on the ship.
    I have few in hand. Sure, I have Thrawn in the ship, but I don’t have Imp Command so battling him is quite risky. I know also that I have 3 Command and the 2 They’ve Shut Down The Main Reactor! in my Force Pile.
    So I decide to Monnok Lost … and he Senses … and he draws Lt Blount for destiny, barely making the Sense … I wished I had my sense too. I would have killed his 2 Commands.
    I add the Laser Cannon Battery, shuttle up B4 and Piett, and draw. I draw the 2 interrupts so I leave 3.

    In his turn he battles, after checking his reserve: he has like 2 6s, 3 5s, and some 3-4 4s .
    He has however Rey aboard, so a 6 and 5 are enough to kill the Executor.
    I play the interrupt basically stopping one of his BD for ability. He does plays Leadership (V), so we both draw 1 BD, but I win on power, so I don’t lose 2.
    He is also setting up for the blow up. I have a EPP Maul in hand but no Ghhhk. And a pull from reserve show both puller and Ghhhk there.
    I just battle again on the system, he loses 1 battle damage. We’re pretty even.
    I draw up to 12.
    In his turn, the bunker blows up and he retrieves his whole lost pile.
    On my next turn, I activate all but 3, and I see that there is a 6, 7 and 2 in reserve. I then add a second Cannon, battle, play again the interrupt to block one of his BDs, and shoot… I draw the 2 … then the 6 … which he makes it a miss thanks to They’re Tracking Us V.
    I’m left with a 7 … which I draw for my only BD, we tie to power.
    There goes my chance at winning the game.

    From there on I do my best to keep him low and I manage to drain a bit and make him deploy more stuff, which I trample some.
    I eventually manages to kill the Home One but only late, when time is called.
    I lose by 18 on time.


    Day 3: my EBO Ketwol vs Jimmy’s Invasion Blaster

    Alas it’s Invasion Blaster.
    The good news is that I get Artoo in Ship turn 1 with AWRI&DS, and I have Tydirium in hand.
    Seeing Squassin as starting effect, Jimmy does what he should do: he just drops a Battle Droid + blaster at the 3/2, to put pressure, and leaves the Naboo ship on its own, which was what I wanted.
    I was very tired (slept 4 hours, too much driving) and I completely misplayed the turn 1.
    I am activating 8. I have Ketwol in hand so I need to drop him in space at Naboo to kill the lone ship, but I need a BD. Luke in ship is 4, and if I drop the Tyridirium first, I risk the free shield and then I’m dead. And total is 8 still so too much.
    I ponder a while thinking about how I can maybe wait a turn … and realise I have Poe in hand, but he’s still 4.
    And this is where I realise also that Ketwol deploys -1 to the Tydirium. So I can drop Ketwol+Poe+Tydirium and there’s nothing he can do!
    So I’m very excited and do that …
    The battle ends up with me drawing 2 BDs, a 5 and 2, so 14 total, so only 1 overflow.
    But I should have done differently … play Ketwol+ship, and seeing he didn’t play a shield, flash Luke instead of adding Poe. I would have drawn only 1 BD but would have had power 12 and not 7, which was way better, and I would have overflown him by 4 and I would have already 2 ships in space which would have been great for not burning more cards and force to spread in space next turn.

    Anyway, the ship goes down in flames.
    The game is just then us draining at 3 sites / systems. But he’s damaging for 3 a turn with the gun effect (while I’m retrieving 1 with Luke). He grabs also Escape Pod Combo of course.
    I think (wrongly) that he has quite a good amount of ground, so I don’t land on a site or deploy on the 4th marker. This mistake costs me the game, by his own words.
    We end up with some last minutes fight on Naboo and also Jyn blocking the 3/2 drain (getting snipered, but she goes to Used to 3PO pulled her again) but in the end I lose last turn by 3.

    So in the end, I’m out, but it was a very difficult game I could have won only by what I did and occupying also a BG site, considering that it’s a very bad match-up (autoloss as far as me and Jimmy think).
    Years ago I knew decklists and what to expect from decks, nowadays I don’t, and this is very important in tournament games.

    I am still very happy to have made Day 3, and played vs Jimmy (they were very fun games, we were laughing and joking about all that was happening).
