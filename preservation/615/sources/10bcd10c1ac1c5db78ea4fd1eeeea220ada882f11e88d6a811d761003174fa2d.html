Tauntaun Handler

"Wilbur" is written into horns of the Tauntaun.

Note: Wilbur was the human caretaker of "Mr. Ed," a television program which aired roughly thirty years ago. Maybe you'll recognize the first verse of the show's theme: "A horse is a horse, of course, of course, and no one can talk to a horse, of course, that is, of course,unless the horse is the famous Mr. Ed."
