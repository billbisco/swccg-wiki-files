Crash Landing

"J. G. Ballard" is printed on the side of the snowspeeder, on the front facing of the metal plating, to the side (underneath) the cockpit.

J. G. Ballard is a reference to author James Graham Ballard, who wrote "Crash" in 1973. "Crash" fits in with the theme of Crash Landing. (Ballard also wrote Empire of the Sun, which could be stretched into a tie in with Empire Strikes Back.)
