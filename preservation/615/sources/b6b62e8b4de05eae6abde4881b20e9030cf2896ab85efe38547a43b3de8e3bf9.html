It Can Wait
I'd Just as Soon Kiss A Wookiee
review by Jeff Hansen
[click here or here to view card]

These unique Lost Interrupts can be quite annoying, and somewhat helpful despite their low destiny numbers. They play if the opponent just deployed a card (except Immediate Effect), using 3 Force to return it to the opponent's hand. The Force they spent remains used, and on the next turn they must redeploy that card (if possible) for free using the same target(s). This is a great way to delay the opponent's strategy for a turn when they try to deploy a very powerful card. That alone can be worth stocking a destiny 2 card in your deck, as it gives you an extra turn to prepare for it. These cards are also great to use in an old-fashioned manipulation deck. Following up with a Grimtaash / Monnok (Used or Lost) or Recoil In Fear / Uncertain Is The Future can get rid of the "bounced" card for much longer than a single turn! If the "bounced card was a character, Scanning Crew, M'iiyoom Onith, or Oola becomes even more effective! These are nice cards that can fill that one empty slot in your deck!
