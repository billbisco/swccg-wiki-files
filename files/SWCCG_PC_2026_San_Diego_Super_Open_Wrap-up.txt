Title: 2026 San Diego Super Open Wrap-up – Joe Olson Conquers
Published: April 28, 2026
Captured by the SWCCG Wiki from the public Players Committee page on 11 October 2026 (article text only).

Star Wars CCG major tournaments in 2026 are continuing strong, with a return to sunny San Diego, CA for the 2026 San Diego Super Open on April 10-12. The weekend event concluded with Joe Olson defeating Daniel Amor in the final confrontation match to take the trophy. This is Team 5 member Joe’s first Major Event victory in 2026, although as a 3-time World Champion, he is no stranger to finishing as a top 8 qualifier at Major Events (including a top 8 in the 2026 Boston Winter Classic event).

SDSO Champion Joe Olson

Rounding out the main event’s top 4 finishers from the super combination of ten games over Day 1 & 2 were also Sam Tashima and Chris Wirfs. The weekend also included a Sunday Retro event won by the undefeated Eddie Szwabowski.

The most popular decks played at the event included a lot of variety across the board for both sides. The decklists page has been updated for the event so check that out to see all the decks and card choices used by every player. Be sure to keep an eye out for player tournament reports posted soon about the weekend over on the Tournament Reports subforum. Thanks to any TR writers who want to share their experiences with the community! Also be sure to go watch some of the stream coverage over on the PC Youtube page, for the weekend.

A massive, special thanks go out to Tournament Advocate Chris Schoenthal for being the Tournament Director and the primary overseer for the weekend. Dan Tartaglione and Garrett Larson deserve a huge round of applause for their awesome streaming and commentary coverage for the weekend. Additionally, a big shout-out goes to especially Karl Koenig for helping organize the event, securing some amazing event space, as well as the local “Basement Crew” also for being warm and welcoming to players from near and far (of all experience levels). Go check out the full listing of people that the Players Committee needed to thank here, and also check out the Event subforum for any pictures and other info recapping the event.
