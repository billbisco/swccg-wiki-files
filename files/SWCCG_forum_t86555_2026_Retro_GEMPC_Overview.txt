Seeding and tournament play
1. Once registration closes, the 2026 Retro GEMPC Selection Committee will convene and rank the participants into a bracket. Some players may have a bye due to uneven player numbers
2.  Once the event begins, players will have 7 days to complete a full match (one game with each side) against the opponent they have been assigned, with the higher-seeded player (the one with the lower seed number) choosing which side of the Force is played first.  Players MUST use the same locked decks throughout the tournament (Minor exception is that the tournament director may on a case by case basis allow early fixes for obviously deficient decks).  
Scoring, Winning, Etc.
1. Each match will follow standard Match Play scoring procedures. The player with the highest net life force (cards in Reserve Deck, Used Pile, and Force Pile) from both of their games wins the  match and proceeds further.  Example: If Abe won Game 1 by 6 Life Force but Bob won Game 2 by 8 Life Force, Bob's net life force from both games would be +2 and Bob would win the Match.  Exception: concessions by the opponent and time-outs by the opponent ALSO cause hand size to count as life force for a player)
2. In case of a tie, the fewest cards across both games in the discard pile are the tiebreaker.  
Example:  
Game 1: Cary wins by 12 life force with 45 cards in Discard while David lost with 48 cards in Lost Pile
Game 2: David wins by 12 life force with 44 cards in Discard while Cary lost with 50 cards in Lost Pile
Total Cards in Lost Pile for Both Games Cary: 45+50 = 95
Total Cards in Lost Pile for Both Games David: 48+44 = 92
Because both players are even in their respective life force totals for their wins, the game is decided by a tiebreaker.  Because David had fewer cards across both games in his lost pile (92) compared to Cary (95),  David wins the tiebreaker and wins the Match.  
Be sure to record both the differential of each game and the cards in each player's lost pile for tiebreakers at the end of each game.
3. Games are played with 50-minute timers per player and the standard 5-minute "inaction" timer. If a player reaches 0:00 on their clock or is inactive for 5 minutes, the game will be forfeited in favor of the opponent. The winner wins by the total of their hand & life Force remaining
4. As this is a match-play event, and differential is being kept, concessions and timeouts are strongly discouraged. If a player does choose to concede or happens to timeout within a game, the winner wins by the total of their hand & life Force remaining. This differential is subject to the Tournament Director's discretion; concessions and timeouts are not intended as a strategic option to lower the differential of a match by preventing a retrieval loop or similar.
5. If a match is tied in Life Force, the player with fewer cards in lost piles across the match will win. If lost pile counts are tied, the lower-seeded player will advance.
6. The player who wins the match, either by winning both games outright or having a higher differential across the two games, will advance to the next round of the bracket.
7. To report the winner of a match, one of the participants should post in the thread on the viewtopic.php?t=86782 - this allows us to easily keep track of all results!
8. Reverts are not required to be allowed.  However, granting reverts for misclicks and minor mistakes is encouraged as an aspect of courteous sportsmanship.
9. Intentionally using known bugs or exploits is heavily frowned upon and may punished at the discretion of the Tournament Director 
10. If a new bug occurs during a game, play it as it lies if possible. If it is extremely disruptive to the game's result, please notify the Tournament Director, along with a game replay if possible.
Prizes, Awards, and Delivery
1. This tournament is being self-funded by Bill Bisco and as such it is a labor and effort of love offered freely to the community and those participating in the hopes that people have fun.  
2. Bill Bisco currently has the funds and the capital to deliver prizes and it is his full intention to start the process shortly after the tournament completes.  
3. The playmat prizes are intended to be customized for the recipients and so that aspect of tournament prize will take at least a couple of weeks as they must be custom ordered, delivered, and shipped.  
4. Given the effort it takes to manage, fund, and deliver these prizes.  Bill Bisco reserves the right to withhold or re-assign prizes from randomly chosen individuals if those individuals are believed (with some evidence) to not have completed their games with integrity.  For example, quickly conceding games, skipping games with no notice, etc.  The participation prizes are meant to reward individuals for signing up and enjoying the game with their fellow players, not as a mechanism to try to roll the dice and have a random chance to get prizes without actually meaningfully participating in the tournament.
5. Random participation prizes are meant to be random and will be chosen randomly.  However, if a player is already a TOP 4 player and will already be receiving prizes, that player will be ineligible for random prizes, and another player will be chosen.
