    Title: 8-is-great-at-ma-states-deck-steebo
    Author: michael "Deck" Gemme
    Date: Apr 16, 2001

    States!
    Holy sugar daddy, states was big - preparation for states was far bigger as me, Steebo Baroni, and Johnny Vegas had all decided to play the same decks a few weeks in advance. I was stuck up at school with no cards other than my decks from my last tournament, so all i could do to get ready was write down tons of stuff in all my classes and ask steebo about situations and stuff while he playtested.

    So i head home the wednesday before states and playtest with steebo, he has made a new dark and a new light he wants to try now, and they work pretty well so we say we will play them. Friday rolls around and we play all day, go to superchinese Buffet and get wasted on egg rolls and soy sauce, and we come up with the name of his tie deck - Tie Rolls and Tallon sauce. Its a tie rolling deck that plays 8 of the combo cards and 4 of each OTSD tie and 4 scythe Ties. We playtest Friday night with Johnny Vegas and his friend Aaron, ultimately deciding to play a better version of Avatar’s QMC, by better i mean heavier in space as we use Two Ten Numbs, two intruder missiles and Rapid fire - and renamed the deck appropriately to "Ten Numb Superstar" after the brangus glee superstar deck (obviously). Then its time for the Brownie.

    I beleive in the brownie, because its all mental.. if you read clint’s articles about the brownie, the concept is all karma. The brownie is karma, and if you eat the brownie, there is your karma. So the four of us show up to dennys and steve has a coupon to get a free brownie from the last time we went before the infamous Playstation 2 tournament. However steve makes a bet with me on the gametext of imperial command for the price of one brownie, and of course he loses becuase i never lose a bet, and i get the free brownie. Now steve decides that he is too good (and too cheap) to buy two brownies (which cost 2.63), so he doesnt get one, and sticks to eating a BLT. Since it was my second experience with the brownie, i took it down easily without aid of a beverage. Johnny and Aaron struggled the whole way, i clearly finished with the cleanest plate and proved to do the best out of all the brownie eaters the next day. On a side note, for a dennys the waitress was bangin! she was like 18 thin, pretty cute, cute voice, really sweet, and had all her teeth! But then again we were in dennys where the other waitresses, including the "toothless wonder" who served us our brownie last time, are terrible looking - so even my hairy @#$% was looking pretty hot to all the citizens eating at dennys at 1am on a friday night.

    Me and steebo prepare our decks and go to bed after talking and stuff. We saw Hayes Hunters decktech page that day and were pissed how @#$%y he was even though he did have the best shot of winning, at least when im @#$%y its wicked funny because i am not expected to win.

    Daylight breaks and we are up at the buttcrack of dawn, print out the official decipher decklists (we needed it’s numbered list becuase steebo got DQ’d at worlds because he cannot count to 60). We fight with my dog as he tries to eat our decklists, our eggo waffles and our four ten numbs, before taking the outrider (my brand new 2k1 navy jetta) to its first tournament ever, and the biggest in our region this year. We show up and friggin jimmEH scheimer was sick and didnt show up so i was pissed becuase he is Puscheimer Thryss and a good kid, funny, and we like him so sorry you couldnt make it jimmEH. But among the players people would recognize are mike scheimer (2000+), Justin H20 Desai (1900+), and Eric Hunter. Steebo is of course a notable, and Hayes was nowhere to be found as he was too busy spiking his hair at home. Me and steebo thought of the great tech of slashing all the hunter’s tires, but then realized that the ultimate tech was that they live 3 minutes walking distance from the venue - and thus would defeat us anyways.. So mrs hunter was glad we decided against slashing tires and putting tacks in the driveway - and then the pairings were called.
