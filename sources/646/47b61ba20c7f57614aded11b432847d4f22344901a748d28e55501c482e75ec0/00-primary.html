    Here is the bracket as selected by the players during the OCS Top 16 Selection Show!
    As a reminder, this bracket was also randomized after the first-round matchups were drafted, hence the odd numbering.
    bracket_oct_2025.png (37.7 KiB) Viewed 962 times
    bracket_oct_2025.png (37.7 KiB) Viewed 962 times

    Playoff rules:
    •Decklists are required to be submitted to decklists@starwarsccg.org for all Playoff matches in GEMP-importable format (.txt) and Deck list format. Please see this post for detailed instructions.
    •The Tournament Committee has agreed upon a revised schedule for decklist submission. Rather than the "16 + 8" and "4+2" format from last year, players will submit their lists one round ahead of the tournament. This allows players to have some knowledge of the potential pool of opponents, but not have perfect knowledge. As such, the schedule will be as displayed at the bottom of this post.
    •Archetypes played will be included in game results, but full decklists for each round will not be posted until after the event concludes (and lists not played, due to the player being eliminated beforehand, will not be posted at all).
    •Each match will follow standard Match Play scoring procedures. If you have any questions regarding these, please let me know. Be sure to record both the differential of each game, and the cards in each player's lost pile for tiebreakers, at the end of each game.
    •Since the bracket is shuffled, there may be matchups that change the ordering/seeding. Regardless of how the players are drafted or the bracket is set up, the higher seed (for the purposes of determining which side is played in which order) is always defined as the player who is the highest remaining on this list.
    •Games are played with 45 minute timers per player, as well as the standard 5-minute 'inaction' timer. If a player reaches 0:00 on their clock, or is fully inactive for 5 minutes, the game will be a forfeit in favor of the opponent. As a reminder, for online events, the timed rules are as follows: If the time-out occurs in game 1 of a match, the winner wins by the total of their hand & life Force remaining; if the time-out occurs in game 2, it counts as a match concession.
    •Players should notify me, either via PM here, Slack, or tournaments@starwarsccg.org, when they have scheduled their games. I will add each game to this post as was done for last year's playoffs.
    •If new cards or errata are released, they will be made legal at the beginning of the next full round, not immediately.
    •Players are not allowed to watch the Twitch stream of their own game while playing.
    •I will be assisted by Blake Silkwood and Justin Miyashiro for this event. We will convene to discuss any potential issues, and if necessary, escalate major ones to the full Tournament Committee (with event participants being asked to recuse themselves.)

    Schedule of Rounds
