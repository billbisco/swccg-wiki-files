Virtual Card Set #1 is Away!

The Star Wars™ Customizable Card Game continues to evolve with the release of the first Virtual Card Set. These cards are specially selected by members of the Gameplay Design team from a pool of cards mostly deemed "useless" or "obsolete" in the eyes of the SWCCG Community. The selected cards are given new and balanced game text to not only make these cards useful once more (some for the first time) in the current tournament environment, but to also encourage and strengthen the competitiveness of storyline gameplay.

This first set is made up of twelve cards – six Light Side and six Dark Side – all taken from the Premiere set of the Star Wars CCG. To use these cards, simply download and print the .pdf file. The game text boxes are inserted into an opaque sleeve with the appropriate card. The card is then inserted into the deck and played as any other card in your deck. Players may choose not to use the virtual text while constructing their deck, however a player may not switch a card either way once the tournament has begun. Players may have both versions of a card – the original and the (V) version – in their deck. It should also be pointed out that anything related to the Virtual cards will be marked with a (V). This includes references to the cards in rulings, deck articles, and deck lists.

The cards from Virtual Card Set #1 will be legal Saturday, March 9th. All sanctioned tournaments are required to allow these cards to retain their "sanctioned" status.

{Printing note: When you print the pdf files, make sure that "expand small pages to paper size is not checked, otherwise the images will be too large to fit your card sleeves.)
