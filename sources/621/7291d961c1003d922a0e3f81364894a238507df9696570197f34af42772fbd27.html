VIRTUAL CARD DESIGN OPPORTUNITIES:
For the second straight year, three virtual card design opportunities are at stake at the Minnesota SWCCG State Championship Weekend.


PRE-REGISTRATIONS:
Pre-registrations for all three tournaments can be submitted via e-mail to nick.kolnik@gmail.com.
Pre-registration e-mails must include: First name, last name, address, city, state, zip code and date of birth.
No payment is required (or even accepted) with your pre-registrations.

I encourage everyone to pre-register, not only to take advantage of the discounted entry fees but because it significantly helps Twin City Comics and myself estimate the number of attendees for each event and thus plan accordingly. Pre-registration-based attendance estimates have been incredibly valuable to us in the past, so if you're planning to attend one or more of these tournament, please submit your pre-registration as early as possible!


DECK LISTS:
Deck lists are mandatory for the state championship tournament and can be downloaded from http://www.swccgpc.com/Resources/swdecklist.pdf Please have your deck lists completely finalized and filled out before you arrive at Twin City Comics on July 29th. The more people who have their deck lists ready, the more likely it is that we'll start on time. Please note that once you submit your deck lists with your tournament registration, you will not be allowed to see your deck lists or to make any changes.


NICK KOLNIK'S RETIREMENT TOURNAMENT:
The 2006 Minnesota SWCCG State Championship will be the last event I run as an active tournament director. Please join me in celebrating the success our playing community has had, particularly since Decipher's loss of the SW license, by attending and competing in the events of this weekend.
