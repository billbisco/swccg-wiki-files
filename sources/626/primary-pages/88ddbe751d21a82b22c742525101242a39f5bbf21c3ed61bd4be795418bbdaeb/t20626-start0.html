    Post by band_member » April 28th, 2008, 11:20 am

    Alright I'm not really going to post a tournament report per se. This is a rambling list of thoughts and reflections on the weekend past with almost no game details but I'm sure those will sneak in. It will also be pg-13 and deal with items of a mature nature.
    1. Texas players are nice. While there are a couple of reasons I decided to make the trip, the major one is that there's not a single Texas guy that I don't think is a nice guy. They all seem very friendly and lack the attitude that sometimes comes with this game. Michael Richards seems like he's nice to almost everyone in the world, well except those dirty illegal immigrants. He hates them with a passion.
    2. Thanks Michael Richards. I wouldn't have come if you didn't personally invite me. You did violate part of our agreement though. I said I'd come if you gave me a ride to and from the airport...check; a place to stay...check; and take me out to eat at a nice Texas restaurant...fail.
    3. Applebees is the best restaurant in Texas. After spending 2 and a half days in Texas, I spend 2 of those days eating dinner at the same Applebees. Apparently, Applebees is the classiest place to eat in Houston and whenever any 'outsiders' come, they get to go there.
    4. Some random quotes from the weekend...
    -I was talking to Hunter and I said something about how Lingrell was asking Garrett Larson how Hunter played the game. Hunter laughed and said, "How I play the game?...Like a MACHINE!" at which point I realized I'm dumb and decided to skulk away and not embarrass myself anymore by talking with Hunter.
    -Joe Olson "Thanks for not cheating." It was so strange I had to repeat it again out loud. I was playing WYS and I rested the whole tournament on whether I had a Dodge in the Lost pile or not. I decided not to risk it and left my 2 smugglers together instead of spreading out. I did NOT look through my lost pile which Joe Olson thought was something special. Well it's in the rules that you can't look and I wouldn't knowingly just violate it. Funny.
    -JW saying "I like your body. Can you come over to my place tonight?" Okay he didn't say that at all.
    5. Jim Sharp makes me uncomfortable. After making repeated hints and suggestions about inappropriate touching, he offers me a drink. Being thirsty, I drank it. I woke up a few hours later remembering nothing but I did notice my underwear was on inside out and a strange tingling in certain extremities.
    6. Preparation is probably a good thing for SWCCG. I had exactly 2 games of SW to prepare for this tournament. Lingrell and Hunter talked about how they prepared and I just felt awkward. I didn't do any. Probably would have helped with my next item.
    7. Not making deck building errors is good. I had a non-virtual cave puller card. I tried to play it to get my cave...oh...there should be some more text on there....oh dang.
    8. Not making game play errors is even better. Well if I wrote anything in here, I'd spend the next hour writing it up. Best to just say I f'd it up and go on.
    9. People apparently think I suck at SW. Never bothered me before but saying I shouldn't even be in the top 10? "What have you done for me lately" I guess.
    10. Matt Lush hates me. Well I thought when I saw the bracket that at least I wouldn't have to play anyone that thinks I'm the devil. Apparently I burn more bridges than I thought. This was back in my old Decktech days, before the banning. I can't remember the details and I don't know if I actually said anything directly to Matt Lush or what. I know Neal Razi hates me and so did Clint Hayes. I also knew that hatred from those guys seemed to spill over and be taken up by other people. Michael Richards told me this as we were driving to the airport to send me home otherwise I probably would have made an effort to make amends or at least remember what I had said to make him hate me. I thought I only had 5 mortal enemies in SWCCG so I hope I don't have a 6th.
    11. Getting away from personal issues to play SW doesn't make those personal issues go away. I thought I'd have a break from real-life issues by getting away for the weekend. This is another reason I wanted to go on this trip. Apparently those issues just don't solve themselves when you leave them alone. Oh well, that's all I'll say about that.
    12. My girlfriend crying the weekend before and telling me to skip this trip entirely. While I am not caring enough to actually skip the trip, it did tell me essentially "Don't playtest at all this whole week and do things with her!" So I had to skip any actual game preparation time.
    13. I spent about 14 hours in planes this weekend. A good portion of that time was spent trying to figure out the logistics of joining the mile-high club. It was more fantasy than reality as I didn't have the essential ingrediant for making that come true but I'd like to think I'd be prepared in case I ever had the opportunity. How would I get into the bathroom unnoticed? Do people do it on these kinds of commercial planes or is it just on like private planes? What sorts of sex acts could I get away with? Is the blanket big enough to cover some fondling in the seats? I spent less than 15 minutes thinking about what I'd do if there were snakes on the plane.
    14. Cedric Simon's choice of music sucks. Randy Travis?
    15. Scott Lingrell over dinner asked me if WTO,AOT effects both players? I'm like "huh" Didn't we spend 2 years working on that card together? It's like when Schele was playing against Brady. Brady puts out WTO,AOT against him and Schele reaches across the table and has to read the card. I yell out, "What? You don't even know what the card does and you spend 30 pages yelling about it?" It's great.
    16. I love it when I accept a deck's weakness and then run into it. At worlds I said, "Okay, this D!ckhat deck isn't that good against Saber Combat but I'll accept that loss." and proceeded to play against 2 Saber Combats. At the Texas MPC I said, "Okay, this BHBM isn't that good against WYS but I'll accept that loss." and proceeded to play against 2 WYS decks.
    17. Headbands and armbands don't make you a better SW player. Sad but true.
    18. JW doesn't really play basketball.
    19. The only one too scared to show up was Scott Church. He needs to be better at lying to his wife.
    20. I'm out. This has taken up enough of my time and the only ones who are going to read it are the 5 I've metioned earlier, Garrett Larson, and Peter Jacobson.
    Last edited by band_member on April 28th, 2008, 12:03 pm, edited 1 time in total.
